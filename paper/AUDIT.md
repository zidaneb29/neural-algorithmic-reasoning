# Scientific audit and paper outline

- **Established**. An attributed rooted ball determines the root output under the stated local update rules. A unit path gives a fixed-depth worst-case and pair-mean absolute error bound. This specializes established message passing.
- **Measured**. Raw error increases across the tested connected random graph sizes. Family errors differ at matched size. Standard depth eight helps at 64 nodes and in the controlled path study. Residuals show no consistent benefit. Endpoint training trades center accuracy for endpoint accuracy.
- **Confounding**. Size changes diameter, reach and target scale. Family changes degree and route redundancy as well. Phase 3 depth changes capacity and work. Phase 4 depth changes width and runtime despite near-matched dense operations. Source placement changes both hop and label support.
- **Hypotheses**. Local structural cues or shifted prediction priors could explain improvements beyond reach. The experiments did not measure their role, algorithm traces, oversmoothing or oversquashing.
- **Units and uncertainty**. Raw errors are in weight units. Normalized MAE is a graphwise ratio. Hop bands pool nodes within replicate. Paired differences precede rounding, and paired sample SD includes both graph draw and training variation.

The paper moves from research questions and prior work to methodology and generalization, then proves locality and the error bound, reports the controlled validation, and closes with interpretation, limitations and reproduction.
